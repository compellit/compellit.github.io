On September 25, the COMPEL project took part in European Researchers' Night in Santiago de Compostela, in the context of [G-NIGHT](https://gnight.gal/), the Galician edition of the event. Our stand was part of the workshops at the Cloister of the Colexio de Fonseca ([program](https://gnight.gal/obradoiros-no-claustro-do-colexio-fonseca/)), with the CiTIUS activity "Humanidades computacionais: cifras e versos" (Computational humanities: verse and numbers).

The activity presented the automatic detection of formal features of Galician poetry based on natural language processing technologies, including large language models, and how it makes it possible to analyze large volumes of text. Visitors interested in the computational analysis of Galician poetry could try out [GALAPAGOS](https://prf2.org/galapagos/). Plus, we had a plush tortoise, the GALAPAGOS mascot.
