[CLARIAH-GAL](https://www.clariah.gal/) is the Galician node of CLARIAH-ES, which coordinates Spain's participation in the European Research Infrastructure Consortia [CLARIN](https://www.clarin.eu/) and [DARIAH](https://www.dariah.eu/). CLARIAH-GAL is managed by [Instituto da Lingua Galega](https://ilg.usc.gal/), in collaboration with CiTIUS, which hosts the COMPEL project.

CLARIAH-GAL organized its third workshop on May 29, 2026. The workshop program is at [https://ilg.usc.gal/gl/actividades/iii-xeira-clariah-gal](https://ilg.usc.gal/gl/actividades/iii-xeira-clariah-gal).

We presented a poster on GALAPAGOS, the [Galician Automatic Poetry Analysis System](/news/galician-automatic-poetry-analysis-system-online/), covering our first results on automatic metrical scansion and enjambment detection for 19th-century Galician poetry.
