We release [**GALAPAvis**](https://galapavis.app.citius/gal) ([https://galapavis.app.citius/gal](https://galapavis.app.citius/gal)), a visualization dashboard to navigate 19th-century poetry corpora in Galician and Spanish; the current **data volume** is ca. 120,000 lines per language. The poems come with automatic **annotations for prosodic features**: metrical scansion, enjambment, rhyme scheme and stanza types, obtained with [tools](/news/galician-automatic-poetry-analysis-system-online/) based on Natural Language Processing (NLP) developed in our project. A rhyme **search** (lexical and phonetic) is also possible.

The dashboard has **interactive charts** that give an aggregated view of how prosodic features are distributed across time or across groups of authors. From the charts, you can access the poems that contain the features selected. This helps **compare authors** or groups of authors, and **prosodic features**.
