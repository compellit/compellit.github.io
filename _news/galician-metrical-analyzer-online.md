The web interface to GAMA, the Galician Metrical Analyzer, is online at **[https://prf2.org/gama/](https://prf2.org/gama/)**. GAMA is under developement at the COMPEL project and performs scansion (metrical analysis) for poetry in Galician. Pauline Moreau, Anxo Alonso and Pablo Ruiz are developing its web interface.
