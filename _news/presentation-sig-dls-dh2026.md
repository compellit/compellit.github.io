SIG-DLS is ADHO's Special Interest Group on Digital Literary Studies. We presented the project and the current state of our corpus and analysis pipeline development at the [SIG's 2026 mini-conference](https://dls.hypotheses.org/2318), which took place within the [DH2026](https://dh2026.adho.org/) conference. We gave an online lightning talk *Putting a minority tradition on the digital comparative map: Galician 19th-century poetry*; you can watch the video below.
