Pedro Barral Silva's internship at CiTIUS starts today. He is a final year student in the English program at Universidade de Santiago de Compostela's [Faculty of Philology](https://www.usc.gal/en/center/faculty-philology). Within the COMPEL project, he is working on encoding Galician poetry material according to the [TEI (_Text Encoding Initiative_)](https://tei-c.org/release/doc/tei-p5-doc/en/html/) guidelines, applying the project's semi-automatic pipeline plus manual verification.
