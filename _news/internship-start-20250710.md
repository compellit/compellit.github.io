Pauline Moreau's internship at CiTIUS starts today. Pauline is a graduate of the University of Strasbourg's [Language Technologies Master's](https://langtech.unistra.fr/formations/master-traduction-et-interpretation-parcours-technologies-des-langues/?tab=presentation) program. She will be working on developing a web interface for GAMA (the Galician Metrical Analyzer, a scansion tool for Galician poetry) and on corpus developement.
