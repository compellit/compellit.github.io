Edited volume *Popular Voices: Computational Analysis of Poetry and Song* just came out. Pablo Ruiz participated as an editor.

The volume was published by the Estonian Literary Museum Scholarly Press. Several of the contributions focus on less commonly studied traditions like Estonian, Hungarian and Finnish. This focus on "smaller" traditions and the computational approach are points in common with the COMPEL project.  

> Mari Väina, Maria-Kristiina Lotman, Anne-Sophie Bories, Pablo Ruiz Fabo, Petr Plecháč & Susanna Mett (Eds.). *Popular Voices: Computational Analysis of Poetry and Song.* Estonian Literary Museum Scholarly Press, 2025, 978-9916-742-78-5. [⟨hal-05442840v1⟩](https://hal.science/hal-05442840v1) [⟨DOI:10.7592/PP2025⟩](https://dx.doi.org/10.7592/PP2025).
