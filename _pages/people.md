## Related projects

[Nós Project](https://nos.gal/gl/proxecto-nos) (no formal link between the projects): The Nós project, for which CiTIUS is a coordinator, is developing state-of-the-art Natural Language Processing and Speech Processing resources for Galician. We have fine-tuned for poetry analysis some of the base models created by the Nós project or related to it, like the [Llama-Carvalho-PT-GL](https://huggingface.co/Nos-PT/Llama-Carvalho-PT-GL) LLM and the [bert-base-gl](https://huggingface.co/marcosgg/bert-base-gl-cased) encoder. We've also collaborated with Nós researchers, e.g. [[here]](http://compellit.github.io/news/paper-accepted-llms4ssh-at-lrec/).
