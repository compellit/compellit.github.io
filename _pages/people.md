## Related projects

[Proxecto Nós](https://nos.gal/gl/proxecto-nos): No formal link exists between our respective projects, but the Nós project, for which CiTIUS is one of the coordinators, is developing state-of-the-art Natural Language Processing and Speech Processing resources for Galician. We have fine-tuned for poetry analysis use-cases some of the resources created by the Nós project or related to it, like [Llama-Carvalho-PT-GL](https://huggingface.co/Nos-PT/Llama-Carvalho-PT-GL) and [bert-base-gl](https://huggingface.co/marcosgg/bert-base-gl-cased). We've also collaborated with Nós researchers (e.g. [[here]](http://localhost:4000/news/paper-accepted-llms4ssh-at-lrec/)).
